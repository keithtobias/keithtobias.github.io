About Me

Autobiography

I was born and raised in Ragak village, Yimbo East ward, Bondo sub-county, Siaya County, Kenya. I did my primary education in Ragak primary school from 2010-2016. I then joined Got Agulu Secondary School for my secondary education from 2017-2020. I excelled in my Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) examinations and joined Moi University to pursue a Bachelor degree of Science in Information Sciences.

I enjoy self-learning new technologies and use them to create solutions to real-world problems.
What motivates me is the desire to make a positive impact in the world through technology. My goal is to use my skills to create innovative solutions that make a difference in people's lives. My vision is to be a leading technology professional who inspires and empowers other young professionals to achieve their full potential.
